The board

Eight files wide and endless ahead. You start with a king on e2 and a knight on g2. There is no checkmate to reach and no way to win — you climb until something takes the crown.

Moving

Drag a piece, or click it and click the square. Pieces move exactly as in chess: no castling, no en passant.

Dot
a legal quiet move
Ring
a legal capture
Yellow
the move just played

Escape or right-click puts a piece back down.

Growing the army

Recruit
when your king or knight captures, that piece type joins at your rear. No other piece recruits.
Conscript
every 8 moves a pawn arrives at the rear, earned or not.
Promote
a pawn promotes 8 ranks from wherever it was born. One queen at a time; further queens arrive as knights.

What is chasing you

The wake
collapses one rank every 3 moves. Anything it reaches is gone and scores nothing.
The fog
you see about 12 ranks ahead. Nothing beyond it can move or capture — but it is waiting.

Standing still is not an option: the wake advances on your move count, so hesitating costs exactly as much as walking.

How the run ends

The king is captured, the king has no legal safe move, or the wake takes him. Check is real — his square glows red.

Scoring

Distance
15 a rank, counted on the king — running a knight ahead earns nothing.
Captures
pawn 1, knight and bishop 3, rook 5, queen 9, a boss king 25.
Streak
the 4th and later take inside one pack is worth half again.
Survivors
a point each for pieces still standing when you fall.

The loop

Jump or step in, steal a body, drop it at the tail, walk the king up, and do not let the wake take the crown. Losing the knight is survivable, and it is meant to hurt.
